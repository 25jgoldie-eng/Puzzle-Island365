The Recall

The facility wasn’t supposed to look like this.

Concrete walls. Fluorescent lights. A faint hum of machines buried somewhere behind the panels.

You stood in the observation chamber, staring at the monitor.

A single red dot pulsed in the center of the screen. Slow. Irregular. Almost alive.

The lead researcher stepped forward and said:

“You. You must go.”

You were handed a pack — food, rope, a penknife, a metal rod, and a sealed envelope with your name on it.

Hours later, through the fog, the island appeared. A single house stood near the shore.

Inside: dust, silence, and a computer terminal with an irregularly flashing green light.

You pressed the power button.
